一、Reading

U6　A Life Full of Mystery? Meet Agatha Christie!

¶1　Do you enjoy reading stories that keep you guessing until the very end? If you do, you have probably heard of Agatha Christie (1890–1976). She was an English author who is known for her detective novels and short-story collections. Because of this, people often call her the Queen of Crime.

¶2　Many of her stories take place in confined spaces. One of her most famous books, Murder on the Orient Express, is set on a train. In addition, And Then There Were None is about mysterious events on a remote island. Both stories include very unexpected endings that shock readers.

¶3　Christie also created some unforgettable characters. For instance, Hercule Poirot, a smart Belgian detective, always notices small details that others miss. Miss Marple, on the other hand, is an elderly woman who solves crimes by using her life experience. These characters changed mystery stories. They proved that detectives don't have to be strong heroes. A sharp mind is more powerful than physical strength.

導言　Agatha Christie wrote many mysteries, and interestingly, she became part of one herself.

¶1　In 1926, Christie's mother died, and her husband wanted a divorce. During this difficult time, she suddenly went missing for 11 days. Many people, from police officers to ordinary citizens, joined the search for her. When her car was found near a lake, the mystery only grew deeper. Finally, Christie was found safe at a hotel. Surprisingly, she had checked in at the hotel under the surname of her husband's mistress.

¶2　People had different ideas about what had happened to her during the 11 days. Her husband claimed she had lost her memory during those days. However, a popular theory suggested that she had disappeared on purpose to punish her cheating husband. This way, she could embarrass him and his mistress or even make the police suspect him of a crime. Even today, no one knows exactly why she vanished. As 2026 marks the 50th anniversary of Christie's death, her stories continue to fascinate readers around the world.

二、架構偵察

📐 Part A 說明結構排序：請依序點選，排出 Part A 的組織順序。

正解順序：①→②→③→④。Part A 走「總說（人物定位）→ 分說一（場景 confined spaces）→ 分說二（角色對比）→ 抽象結論（A sharp mind…）」。訊號詞依序是 Because of this ／ In addition ／ on the other hand ／ They proved that。常見錯誤：把「角色」段誤放在「場景」段之前，但 ¶3 的 also 已標示它是「再加一項」，不能排第一。

🕒 Part B 時間軸排序（5 句）：請依事件發生順序點選。

正解順序：母親過世、丈夫要求離婚 → 失蹤 11 天 → 汽車在湖邊被發現 → 在旅館被尋獲（冒用 mistress 的姓入住）→ 出現兩種解釋且至今無解。解題程序：先抓時間訊號 In 1926 / During this difficult time / When… / Finally / Even today；再用過去完成式（had checked in）確認「更早的過去」不等於「敘述順序」。常見錯誤：看到 had checked in 就把它排到最前面——它是敘事中的回溯，不是第一個被說出的事件。

🧩 摘要重組（Passage 1）：請依「主題句／支持論點／結論句」的順序點選三句。

摘要 (24 words)：Agatha Christie, the Queen of Crime, set stories in confined spaces. She created unforgettable characters. A sharp mind is more powerful than physical strength.
（第 1 句＝主題句；第 2 句＝支持論點；第 3 句＝結論句）
判斷程序：含「人物＋稱號＋全篇範圍」者為主題句；含 She created 具體事實者為支持論點；不含專有名詞、語氣拉高到通則者為結論句。

🧩 摘要重組（Passage 2）：請依「主題句／支持論點／結論句」的順序點選三句。

摘要：In 1926, Christie went missing for 11 days. Many people joined the search. Even today, no one knows exactly why she vanished.
（第 1 句＝主題句；第 2 句＝支持論點；第 3 句＝結論句）
判斷程序：帶時間點 In 1926 的句子起頭；Many people joined… 是事件的延伸細節；Even today 把時間拉回現在，是收尾標記。

三、四層次思考（English A2–B1／中文解說）

四、學測題型

🔗 混合題 1（架構分析）：In Part A, the writer first tells us who Christie was, then talks about her settings, and then about her characters. Why does the writer put the sentence "A sharp mind is more powerful than physical strength." at the very end?

參考答案：The writer puts it at the end because of the fact that it turns two examples into one general idea, so it works as the conclusion of the whole part.
解說：這一句不含任何專有名詞，是把 Poirot 與 Miss Marple 兩個具體例子「抽象化」的通則句，因此只能放結尾。鷹架 the fact that 後面必須接完整子句（S+V），不可只接名詞片語。常見錯誤：寫成 because of the fact of it turns…（of 後不能接子句）。

🔗 混合題 2（代名詞回指）：Read these lines: "These characters changed mystery stories. They proved that detectives don't have to be strong heroes." Who or what does They refer to? Answer in English and give one clue from the text.

參考答案：They refers to Hercule Poirot and Miss Marple (= these characters). One clue is the fact that the sentence before it uses "These characters," and the two detectives are the only people named in that paragraph.
解說：代名詞回指的解題程序：先找「最近的複數名詞組」→ 再確認語意是否相容（能「證明」事情的是角色，不是 stories）。常見錯誤：誤指 mystery stories，但故事本身不會 prove 什麼，是角色的設定推翻了舊觀念。

🈺 翻譯 1：這位偵探總是注意到別人忽略的小細節。

The detective always notices small details that others miss.
解說：「注意到」用 notice；「別人忽略的」用關係子句 that others miss 修飾 details，miss 在此是「未注意到」而非「想念」——此為 polysemy 考點。

🈺 翻譯 2：即使到了今天，也沒有人確切知道她為什麼消失。

Even today, no one knows exactly why she vanished.
解說：no one 為單數主詞，動詞用 knows；why 引導的名詞子句採「直述句語序」she vanished，不可寫成 why did she vanish。

填空式翻譯

1. 雖然梅森的夢想看似遙不可及，但他每天仍持續努力。

Although Mason's dream seems , he keeps working hard every day.

✅ 答案：Although Mason's dream seems out of reach, he keeps working hard every day.
📘 出處：U2
🔍 解析：片語 out of reach 表「遙不可及」，可譬喻式地描述某事物（夢想、目標或機會等）在能力範圍外，所以達不到；也可以用字面解讀，形容某物在物理上碰不到（太高、太遠等）。常見用法為 beV/seem/feel out of reach。也可在片語後方加上 for sb 或是寫為 out of sb's reach，表示「對某人來說遙不可及」。

2. 請準時完成你的作業，並在明天交給老師。

Please finish your homework and give it to the teacher tomorrow.

✅ 答案：Please finish your homework on time and give it to the teacher tomorrow.
📘 出處：U3
🔍 解析：片語 on time 表示按表定時間的「準時」，常用於上課、交作業、開會、搭車等有明確時間點的情境。常見搭配詞為 beV/V on time 表示「準時到達／做某事」。注意不要與 in time 搞混，該片語強調可能趕不上的「沒錯過；來得及」。

引導式翻譯

3. 泰里斯熬夜到很晚，因此他沒有錯過凌晨兩點的籃球賽。

Tyrese stayed up late, the 2 a.m. basketball game.

✅ 答案：Tyrese stayed up late, so he didn't miss out on the 2 a.m. basketball game.
📘 出處：U4
🔍 解析：由於同一件事的前半句使用過去簡單式 stayed，故後半句也應使用過去簡單式。片語 miss out on sth 為不可分離的動詞片語，意思是「錯失某物」，通常用於描述一個原本可以享受、受益、參與的好機會、好經驗、好東西，帶有明顯的惋惜感。題目句子寫到「因此他沒有錯過」，故使用否定式，即 so he didn't miss out on。

4. 我們支付的價格可能會根據飯店房間的大小而改變。

The price we pay may change .

✅ 答案：The price we pay may change depending on the size of the hotel room.
📘 出處：U5
🔍 解析：介系詞片語 depending on sth 意思是「取決於…；根據…」，後方須接上名詞（片語）。該片語常用於說明某件事會受到某個條件影響，並因此產生不同結果。它在邏輯上類似 if，但特別適合表達「有多種可能性」的情境。題目中名詞片語「飯店房間的大小」寫為 the size of the hotel room。

整句翻譯

5. 會議室上週五由尚恩（Sean）整理乾淨了。

✅ 答案：The meeting room was tidied up by Sean last Friday.
📘 出處：U1（被動語態）＋ U3（tidy sth up）
🔍 解析：題目提到「上週五」和「整理乾淨了」，可知此句是描述過去發生且已經結束的事，因此應使用過去簡單式。透過「會議室由尚恩整理乾淨了」這個陳述方式可知，邏輯上的「受事者（the meeting room）」變成了句子的「主詞」——因會議室自己不會拿掃把去打掃，它只能「接受」別人的整理，故本句應使用被動語態，句型為 S + beV + p.p. (+ by O) 表「某人／物被（某人／物）…」。因句子時態「上週五」為過去式，且句子主詞為單數名詞 the meeting room，故 beV 須用 was。講述「收拾／整理…」可使用片語 tidy sth up，sth 為代名詞時可置於 tidy 跟 up 中間；而動詞 tidy 的過去分詞為 tidied。句中被整理的對象為會議室（the meeting room），置於主詞位置；執行整理動作的人為尚恩（Sean），置於 by 一字後方，句子按照上方公式可寫為 The meeting room was tidied up by Sean。「上週五」這個時間副詞置於句尾，故本句完整寫為 The meeting room was tidied up by Sean last Friday。

6. 截至本學期，這個班上有三分之二的學生在放學後上額外的英文課。

✅ 答案：As of this semester, two-thirds of the students in this class take extra English lessons after school.
📘 出處：U2（as of sth）＋ U5（分數的寫法與讀法）
🔍 解析：從題目中的「截至本學期」可知，句子是在描述到現在仍成立的情況，因此使用現在簡單式陳述。若要表示「截至某時間為止（的統計或狀態）」，可使用片語 as of sth。as of 後方須接上明確時間點，「截至本學期」則寫為 as of this semester。英文中，分數的寫法為分子用「基數」，分母用「序數」，兩者通常用連字號連接。基數指的是 one、two、three …；而序數則是指 first、second、third … 等。在分子大於 1 時，分母的序數須以複數表示。因此，句中的「三分之二」寫為 two-thirds。當分數引導名詞片語作主詞時，後方的動詞單複數取決於分數後方的名詞。句中「這個班上三分之二的學生」寫為 two-thirds of the students in this class，而 the students in this class 為複數，故後方須使用複數動詞，所以「上額外的英文課」寫為 take extra English lessons。「放學後」這個時間副詞置於句尾，故本句完整寫為 As of this semester, two-thirds of the students in this class take extra English lessons after school。

📌 Language in Focus｜非限定用法之同位語

「同位語」為緊鄰某一名詞（片語）或代名詞的相等語，可以是名詞、名詞片語，或是名詞子句。同位語有限定與非限定（亦稱補述）兩種用法，非限定用法之同位語用來補充說明先行詞（即前方緊鄰之詞語），該同位語即使去除也不影響句意理解。須注意，非限定用法的同位語置於句中時，前後須加逗點與句子其他部分隔開：

• One of her (Agatha Christie's) most famous books, Murder on the Orient Express, is set on a train.（課文第 5~6 行）

• For instance, Hercule Poirot, a smart Belgian detective, always notices small details that others miss.（課文第 9~10 行）

• Taipei 101, a famous building in Taiwan, is popular with many foreign visitors.
台北 101——臺灣一棟有名的建築——受到許多外國遊客歡迎。

• Terry, my best friend from school, often helps me with my English homework.
泰瑞——我在學校最好的朋友——常常在英文作業上幫忙我。

非限定用法的同位語可視為非限定形容詞子句之簡化，故第 5~6 和第 9~10 行課文亦可改寫為：

• One of her most famous books, which is Murder on the Orient Express, is set on a train.

• For instance, Hercule Poirot, who is a smart Belgian detective, always notices small details that others miss.

🕵️ Agatha Christie's Masterpieces

Both of these books are the most famous masterworks by Agatha Christie, the Queen of Crime. Murder on the Orient Express is world-famous for its shocking group-crime ending. And Then There Were None is a legendary masterpiece that created the 'isolated island' format (also called the 'stormy mountain villa' style) and the classic 'nursery rhyme murders.'

一、Core Comparison of the Works

FeaturesMurder on the Orient Express (1934)And Then There Were None (1939)
Main DetectiveHercule PoirotNo Detective (Everyone is both a suspect and a victim)
Main SettingThe luxury compartments of the Orient Express trapped in the snowThe helpless and isolated Soldier Island off the Devon coast
Crime PatternA single murder inside a locked and closed train compartmentA series of planned murders following an old nursery rhyme
Core ThemeThe conflict between law and justice, and vigilante justiceCrime and punishment, and unavoidable karma
Classic LabelsTrain room mystery, all-star suspectsIsolated island style, the original nursery rhyme murder book

二、Key Book Summaries

Murder on the Orient Express: The story follows the famous detective Poirot on a luxury train. The train gets stuck in deep snow during the trip. Then, a rich businessman is found stabbed to death inside his locked compartment. Poirot must find the real killer among 13 different passengers on the same train.

And Then There Were None: Ten strangers with secret past crimes are invited to a big house on an isolated island. There is no host on the island. There is only an old nursery rhyme and ten little soldier dolls. Following the words of the rhyme, the guests die one by one, and the killer is hidden among them.

四、延伸寫作

✍️ Use the table above. Write 3–4 sentences to compare the two books. You must use One big difference is that… and On the other hand,….

參考寫法：Both books take place in confined spaces, so no one can leave. One big difference is that Murder on the Orient Express has a famous detective, Hercule Poirot, who looks for the killer. On the other hand, And Then There Were None has no detective at all; everyone is both a suspect and a victim. In addition, the first book asks what justice really means, while the second one is about crime and punishment.
評分重點：(1) 先給「相同點」再給「相異點」，符合 compare-and-contrast 的組織結構；(2) One big difference is that 後必須接完整子句；(3) On the other hand 一定要有前一手，不可放在段落第一句。
